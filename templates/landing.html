Picked from the news

Headlines from science news, Hacker News and Mastodon are collected through the week. Claude groups them into research topics and ranks them by how widely they are covered and how much they are looked up.

From the study to the field

For each topic Claude finds the study behind the coverage and designs an OpenAlex search for the field around it, testing it against OpenAlex until the set is on topic and contains the study.

Mapped by the pipeline

The papers are embedded with SPECTER2 and clustered into a hierarchy of topics, and their citations are traced over time: the topic landscape and the citation network.

Paper data: OpenAlex (CC0). Headlines belong to the outlets named, linked to the original. Source code · API
